Rabbit Hole Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026

Rabbit Hole is a creator platform designed to help users prepare, optimize, manage, and publish original content across supported social media platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when users interact with Rabbit Hole.

Information We Collect

Rabbit Hole may collect account information such as a user's name, profile information, connected social media account identifiers, and authorization information provided by supported platforms.

Rabbit Hole may also process content that users choose to upload, including videos, images, titles, captions, hashtags, thumbnails, and related publishing information.

Connected Social Accounts

When a user chooses to connect a supported social media account, Rabbit Hole uses the platform's official authorization process. Rabbit Hole only requests permissions needed to provide features selected by the user.

Rabbit Hole does not ask users to provide their social media passwords directly. Authentication is handled through the applicable social media platform.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide content optimization, account connection, content preparation, publishing, scheduling, analytics, recommendations, and other creator tools offered by Rabbit Hole.

Content Publishing

Rabbit Hole does not publish content to a connected social account unless the user initiates the publishing action or explicitly schedules content for publication.

AI and Content Analysis

Rabbit Hole may analyze user-provided content to generate recommendations such as titles, captions, hashtags, thumbnails, content classifications, and launch suggestions. Uploaded content may be processed by systems or service providers necessary to provide these features.

Data Sharing

Rabbit Hole may share information with service providers and social media platforms only as necessary to operate requested features, provide authorized publishing services, maintain security, or comply with applicable law.

Rabbit Hole does not sell users' personal information.

Data Security

Rabbit Hole uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information. Access credentials and platform authorization tokens should be stored securely and used only for authorized functionality.

Account Disconnection and Data Requests

Users may disconnect supported social accounts from Rabbit Hole. Users may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information maintained by Rabbit Hole, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Third-Party Platforms

Use of TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and other third-party services is also governed by the privacy policies and terms of those respective platforms. Rabbit Hole is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of those services.

Children's Privacy

Rabbit Hole is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the applicable services in their jurisdiction. Rabbit Hole does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Rabbit Hole develops. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Rabbit Hole support contact provided through the application or official Rabbit Hole website.
